# Real photos

Save the brand's photos in this folder with these exact filenames and they
will appear on the site automatically (until then, stock fallbacks show):

| Filename               | Photo                                                    | Where it shows            |
| ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------- |
| `jollof-spaghetti.jpg` | Plate of jollof spaghetti with peppered chicken (in hand) | Hero (big circle, top)    |
| `party-trays.jpg`      | Six black trays of spaghetti + fried fish + egg           | Our Story section         |
| `noodle-packs.jpg`     | Noodle takeaway packs with boiled eggs and beef           | Gallery: Peppered Noodles |
| `celebration-cake.jpg` | Tall cream cake with pink roses and gold pearls           | Gallery: Celebration Cakes |
| `small-chops.jpg`      | Box of puff puff, samosas, spring rolls, peppered beef    | Gallery: Suya & Small Chops |
| `surprise-box.jpg`     | Birthday surprise box with cake, cupcakes, chops, Chivita | Gallery: Surprise Packages |

Also supported (see src/lib/site.ts):

- `logo.png` in /public (one level up) for the original logo artwork.

Tip: keep files under ~500 KB each for fast mobile loading (crop to roughly
square for the circular frames; 900x900 is plenty).
